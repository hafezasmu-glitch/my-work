Our Story

Two loves, one address.

Wasem began as a single espresso machine and a shelf of secondhand novels in 2016. A decade on, the shelf became a room, the room became two floors, and the espresso machine is still the first thing switched on every morning. We roast in small batches and shelve in careful order — because a good cup and a good chapter deserve the same patience.

Every table here is built for lingering. Stay for one coffee or three chapters — either way, we'll keep the pot warm.
